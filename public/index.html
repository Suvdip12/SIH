// ABOUT

What is SIH?

Smart India Hackathon is a nationwide initiative to provide students a platform to solve pressing problems and foster innovation. University of Kalyani invites all students to participate.

🖥️

Format

Software & Hardware Editions

👥

Team Size

Up to 6 Members per team

👩‍💻

Diversity

At least 1 female member required

🎯

Goal

Select a problem statement & build your solution

🏆

Selection

Internal Hackathon → SIH Finals

📅

Deadline

26th August, 2026

⚠️ Important Instructions

  • Each SIH team can have up to 6 members.
  • At least one female member should be included in every team.
  • Ensure that all information entered is correct and complete.
  • Teams selected through the Internal Hackathon will proceed to the SIH process.
  • All team members must be currently enrolled at the University of Kalyani.
